How to Use Diet Tracker
Quick guide for new users
1
☰ Open the Sidebar
Tap the ☰ button (top-left) to navigate between all 6 sections: Checklist, Calories, Activity, History, Health and Settings.
2
✅ Daily Checklist
Start here every morning. Tick each habit as you complete it — morning ritual, meals, snacks and post-dinner walk. Calories auto-add when you tick food items!
3
🔥 Log Calories
Tap ⚡ Quick Add for common foods (Roti, Dal, Rice). Use the search bar or select a category from the dropdown. Tap 📷 Photo Scan to let AI identify your food from a photo.
4
📷 Photo Scan Setup
Go to ⚙️ Settings → add your free Gemini API key from aistudio.google.com. Then take photos of your food for approximate AI-based calorie estimates. Results are indicative only — adjust manually if needed.
5
🏥 Health Conditions
Have diabetes, BP, thyroid or PCOD? Go to Health tab → tap your condition. Your Checklist will automatically switch to condition-specific meals and habits!
6
💧 Activity Tracking
Log water intake, steps and exercises in the Activity tab. Your calorie burn updates the net calories in real time.
7
⚙️ Settings & Sync
Sign in with Google to save your data permanently in the cloud — works on any device. Export data as CSV anytime for backup.
💡 Tip: Install this app on your home screen! Tap your browser menu → "Add to Home Screen" → it works like a real app with no Play Store needed.
